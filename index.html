How to use this calendar

Three steps to find what is relevant to your business

01

Filter what matters to you

Narrow the year by scope, event type, theme or how you can participate — or reset at any time to see everything.

02

Explore the year

Scan the full year on the Timeline, or switch to List view and browse month by month, in chronological order.

03

Register or learn more

Open any engagement for full details, then register where registration is open, or learn more about what is coming.

The Ten Principles' four pillars

Every engagement below is colour-coded by theme, mapped to the four areas of the Ten Principles of the UN Global Compact. Select a tile to jump straight to that theme in the calendar.
